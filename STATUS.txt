2026-10-28  0.0.47  First headset test, part 4: the real reason for the black headset - the eye images' format
    - 0.0.46 seen working: the trigger on CONTINUE loaded the park (no keyboard or mouse input in between), and
      SteamVR's sync errors stopped. The headset (and SteamVR's VR View) still showed black in the park.
    - SteamVR's OpenXR log for the game (Steam/logs/xrclient_JWE3.txt) says why, every frame: "ComposeLayerProjection:
      failed to submit view 0/1: VRCompositorError_TextureUsesUnsupportedFormat". SteamVR's scene compositor does not
      take the game's 10-bit backbuffer format (R10G10B10A2, DXGI 24) for eye images; its overlays (quad layers: the
      menu screen, dot, beam) do, which is why only those ever showed. xrEndFrame still returns success.
    - Fix: the eye swapchains are created in an 8-bit format SteamVR offers (RGBA8 UNORM first; the full offered list
      is logged), and each eye's texture is drawn into the acquired image by a small fullscreen pass (pixel shader
      loads the texel, alpha 1; compiled at first use with Windows' d3dcompiler_47.dll, recorded on the mod's own
      command list). An sRGB target gets linearised values. Not yet seen in the headset.
    - Lesson: SteamVR's per-app OpenXR log (xrclient_<exe>.txt) and vrcompositor.txt are where a silently dropped
      layer shows up; check them first when OpenXR reports success but the headset is black.
